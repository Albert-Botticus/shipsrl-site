Ohio to Chicago

Ohio to Chicago freight, next day.

Chicago is the biggest freight market in the Midwest and one of our steadiest lanes. We move truckloads from Ohio into Chicagoland every week, from the north suburbs like Glenview down through Joliet, Bolingbrook, and the I-55 warehouse corridor. It is a short run with a lot of trucks on it, which is good for your rate as long as the broker knows the market.

350 MI

Short haul, one driver

Columbus to Chicago is about 350 miles, Toledo to Chicago about 250. Pick up in the afternoon, deliver the next morning.

WEEKLY

Steady volume both ways

We book freight out of Chicago as well as into it, so carriers who take your load have a way home. That keeps them coming back and keeps rates fair.

DOCKS

We know the receivers

Appointment-only distribution centers, lumper fees, and the north suburban industrial parks. We tell the carrier what to expect before dispatch.

Why use a broker on a lane this common

Because the cheapest truck is not always the one that shows up.

Ohio to Chicago has plenty of capacity most weeks, and plenty of carriers who will take a cheap load and then fall off it when something better comes along. We book carriers we have used before, confirm the driver the night before, and track the load so you are not calling us to ask where it is.

When Chicago gets tight, usually around holidays and bad weather, our regular carriers take care of us first. That is the whole point of running the same lane with the same people.

Questions shippers ask

Straight answers

How much is a truckload from Ohio to Chicago?

Send both ZIPs and the pickup date and we will quote from recent loads. Rates on this lane move with the day of the week and the time of month, so a real quote beats an estimate.

Can you pick up today?

Often, yes, if we hear from you in the morning. We have carriers positioned in Ohio most days.

Do you deliver to the Chicago suburbs?

Yes. Glenview, Elk Grove Village, Bolingbrook, Joliet, Romeoville, Aurora, and the rest of the corridor are all regular stops for us.

What about LTL to Chicago?

For a few pallets, LTL is usually the better buy. Use our LTL quote page and you will have carrier rates back in about an hour.
