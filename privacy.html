Privacy Notice

This notice explains what information shipsrl.com collects, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. We keep it short and in plain language.

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Who we are

SRL Logistics LLC ("SRL", "we", "us") is an independent freight agency based in Central Ohio. Brokerage services are provided under the authority of Armstrong Transport Group, Inc. (MC 555609), which is the broker of record on every shipment we arrange. This notice covers the website at shipsrl.com and the quote requests you send through it.

Information you give us

When you submit a quote request or contact form, we collect what you type into it. Depending on the form, that can include:

  • Your name, company, email address, and phone number.
  • Shipment details such as origin and destination zip codes, ship date, freight class, weight, dimensions, piece counts, accessorial needs, and any notes you add.
  • Anything you send us afterward by email or phone while we work on your request.

You do not need to create an account, and we never ask for payment card numbers, bank details, or government ID numbers through this website.

Information collected automatically

  • Analytics. We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how many people visit, which pages they view, and where traffic comes from (for example search, social media, or an ad). Google Analytics sets cookies and collects your IP address, approximate location, browser and device type, pages visited, and a random identifier. We do not send your name, email, or form contents to Google Analytics.
  • Advertising measurement. Our Google Analytics property is linked to our Google Ads account so we can see whether an ad led to a quote request. We record only that a quote was submitted and the form type, never the contents of the form.
  • Hosting logs. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs (IP address, time, requested page) for security and operations.
  • Fonts. Pages load the Inter typeface from Google Fonts, which means Google receives your IP address when the font is requested.

How we use it

  • To price, book, and track the freight you ask us about, and to answer your questions.
  • To tender booked loads to motor carriers and to Armstrong Transport Group for dispatch, billing, and compliance.
  • To understand which pages and channels bring visitors, so we can improve the site and our advertising.
  • To protect the site and our systems from abuse.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for anything unrelated to arranging freight and running this website.

Who we share it with

  • Armstrong Transport Group, Inc. Our broker of record, which handles carrier contracts, payments, and insurance for every load.
  • Motor carriers. Pickup and delivery contact details and shipment information needed to move your freight.
  • Formspree. The service that delivers website form submissions to our email. Formspree processes the submission on our behalf and stores a copy in its dashboard for a limited time.
  • Google. Google Analytics, Google Ads, and Google Fonts, as described above, and Google Workspace for our email.
  • Legal. When required by law, subpoena, or to protect our rights and safety.

Cookies and your choices

The only cookies this site sets are from Google Analytics. You can block them by turning off cookies in your browser, by installing the Google Analytics opt-out add-on, or by using a browser extension that blocks trackers. The site works the same either way. You can also read how Google uses data from sites like ours at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

How long we keep it

Quote requests and shipment records are kept as long as needed to serve you and to meet transportation recordkeeping and tax requirements, which is typically several years after a shipment. Google Analytics data is retained for 14 months. If you ask us to delete information we no longer need, we will.

Your rights

You can ask us what information we have about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Residents of states with privacy laws (including California, Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, and others) may have additional rights, and we will honor them. To make a request, email SRLteam@armstrongtransport.com or call 614-855-9504. We will respond within 30 days and will not treat you differently for asking.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Form submissions travel encrypted to Formspree and then to our email. Access to quote requests is limited to the SRL team and Armstrong personnel who need it to handle your shipment. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect what you send us.

Children

This site is for businesses and is not directed at anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this notice

If we change how we handle information, we will update this page and the date at the top. Material changes will be noted on this page for at least 30 days.
