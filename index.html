Services

Capacity for the freight you ship every week, and the loads that come out of nowhere.

North American truckload coverage through Armstrong's carrier network, with a dedicated operations team you reach directly. If it needs something else, tell us and we will find it.

FTL

Full truckload

Dry van, 53-foot, single and multi-stop. Spot quotes the same day, contract rates on the lanes you run all year.

LTL

Less than truckload

Pallet shipments that do not fill a trailer, priced through Armstrong's LTL partners with one point of contact for the whole move. Get an LTL quote.

DED

Dedicated and recurring lanes

Weekly volume on the same lane? We lock in carriers who know your dock, your hours, and your product.

PRJ

Project and surge freight

Who we work with

Shippers who need the freight to show up, not a sales pitch.

  • Appliance and consumer durable manufacturers. High-volume outbound from Midwest plants to distribution centers and retail.
  • Industrial and building products. Palletized, heavy, and scheduled. We know what a tight dock window costs you.
  • Freight forwarders and 3PLs. Domestic legs for your import and export moves, with the paperwork handled right.
  • Growing businesses. If you are shipping a few loads a month and tired of guessing on rates, we are glad to help.

How it works

From quote to delivered, in four steps.

No portals to learn and no contracts to sign before your first load. Send us the shipment details and we take it from there.
