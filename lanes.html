Lanes

Lanes we run every week.

How we price a lane

Numbers from real loads, not a rate index.

We quote from what carriers have actually accepted on the lane in recent weeks, adjusted for the day of the week, the receiver, and what the fuel market is doing. That is why our quotes tend to hold when the truck is booked.

On lanes we run often we can also offer a standing rate for a set period, which makes budgeting easier for you and planning easier for the carriers.

What a quote needs

  • Origin and destination city and ZIP
  • Pickup date and any delivery appointment
  • Weight, pallet count, and commodity
  • Equipment: dry van, reefer, flatbed
  • Anything unusual: liftgate, live unload, driver assist
