Ohio to Pennsylvania and New Jersey

Ohio to Pennsylvania and New Jersey freight.

Eastern Pennsylvania and New Jersey hold the biggest warehouse concentration on the East Coast, and a lot of Ohio freight moves there on its way to the Northeast. We run this lane every week: the Scranton and Lehigh Valley distribution centers, Philadelphia, central New Jersey, and the port. Nearly 300 loads since 2024 from Ohio shippers.

I-80

Scranton and the Poconos

Covington Township, Tobyhanna, and the I-80 and I-81 distribution belt. About 450 miles from central Ohio, delivered next morning.

NJ

The Jersey warehouse belt

Edison, Cranbury, Carteret, and the Turnpike exits in between. Appointment-heavy receivers, which we book for before the truck is dispatched.

Port of Newark drayage support

Container loading and port deliveries need the right paperwork and timing. We have carriers who do it every day.

Short lanes still need a real broker

Half a day of driving, and a hundred ways to lose it at the dock.

Ohio to Pennsylvania is a quick lane, which tempts shippers to book the cheapest truck on a load board. The problem is never the drive. It is the receiver in New Jersey that wants an appointment two days out, the lumper fee nobody mentioned, and the carrier who will not sit for a live unload. We handle all of that up front and put it on the rate confirmation.

Because our New York and New Jersey freight runs on the same carriers, we can offer a multi-stop or a pair of loads when that fits your schedule, and we book the return freight so the rate stays reasonable.

Questions shippers ask

Straight answers

Can you deliver same day from Ohio to Pennsylvania?

From eastern Ohio into western and central Pennsylvania, often yes. Eastern Pennsylvania and New Jersey are normally next-day.

Do you handle New Jersey appointment deliveries?

Yes. Most of the big distribution centers require them, and we book the appointment before dispatch so the driver is not turned away.

What about Pittsburgh?

Pittsburgh is a three-hour run from Columbus and one of the easiest lanes we cover. Same-day is common.

Can you do a multi-stop into New Jersey and New York?

Yes. Tell us the stops and we will quote a single truck with the sequence that makes sense.
