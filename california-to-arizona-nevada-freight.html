California to Arizona and Nevada

California to Arizona and Nevada freight.

Out of the Inland Empire, two lanes run like clockwork: east on I-10 and I-40 into Arizona, and north on I-15 into Nevada. Our team has booked about 700 truckloads on them since 2024, to Flagstaff, Phoenix, Mesa, and Tempe on the Arizona side and to Las Vegas, North Las Vegas, and Sparks on the Nevada side. Stockton to Reno and Sparks over Donner Pass is part of the same carrier network.

AZ

Phoenix and Flagstaff

Perris to Phoenix is about 330 miles, to Flagstaff about 450. Next-morning delivery with a single driver. Flagstaff in winter needs a carrier who chains up, and ours do.

NV

Las Vegas and Reno

Inland Empire to Las Vegas is a 250-mile run we cover same day or next day. Stockton to Sparks and Reno is a regular Northern California lane for us as well.

700

Loads on these lanes

Roughly 350 loads to Arizona and 350 to Nevada since 2024. The carriers who run them for us are based on both ends, so there is almost always a truck.

Desert lanes, handled

Heat, grades, and receivers that close early.

Arizona and Nevada receivers tend to run early and close early, and summer heat changes what can sit on a trailer. We book delivery appointments that fit the receiver's hours, confirm the driver the evening before, and track the load so you know when it will arrive without calling anyone.

Because freight also comes back out of Phoenix and Las Vegas, our carriers do not need a premium to go east. That keeps your rate close to the lane average even in produce season when California capacity tightens.

Questions shippers ask

Straight answers

What does a truckload from California to Phoenix cost?

Send the ZIPs and the pickup date and we will quote from recent loads. The lane is dense, so rates are usually reasonable unless it is a Friday afternoon pickup.

Can you deliver to Las Vegas next day?

Yes. From the Inland Empire it is often same day. From the Bay Area or the Central Valley it is next day.

Do you serve Flagstaff year round?

Yes, including winter. We use carriers who are equipped for the I-40 grades and chain requirements.

What about Reno from Northern California?

Stockton, Tracy, and Sacramento to Reno and Sparks is a regular lane for us, with carriers used to the pass.
