Ohio to the Southeast

Ohio to Georgia, Florida, and the Carolinas.

Atlanta, the Florida markets, and the Carolinas are where a lot of Ohio manufacturing ends up. We run these lanes every week with carriers who are based in the Southeast and want freight home, which is the cheapest truck you can find. Hundreds of loads south since 2024, from Columbus, Clyde, Marion, and the Cleveland area.

ATL

Atlanta and north Georgia

McDonough, the I-75 and I-85 warehouse belt, and the Atlanta DCs. About 550 to 650 miles from central Ohio, one-day transit.

FL

Florida, all of it

Jacksonville, Orlando, Tampa, and South Florida. Two-day transit on most lanes, and we know which receivers in Florida need an appointment days ahead.

NC

Carolinas

Charlotte, the Triad, Raleigh, and the upstate of South Carolina. A quick run down I-77 that we cover with regional carriers.

Backhaul is the whole game going south

Southeast carriers want to go home. We give them a reason.

Freight going into the Southeast is plentiful, and rates reward brokers who can keep a carrier loaded both directions. Because Armstrong Transport Group books freight out of Georgia, Florida, and the Carolinas every day, our carriers rarely deadhead back. That is why our southbound rates hold up when the spot market gets strange.

Reefer is common on this lane for food and temperature-sensitive goods headed into Florida. We book reefers through the same vetted carrier base and confirm temperature settings on the rate confirmation.

Questions shippers ask

Straight answers

What does a truckload from Ohio to Atlanta cost?

Send the ZIPs and date and we will quote from recent loads. Atlanta is a dense market, so the rate is usually sensible as long as the pickup is not a Friday afternoon.

How long does Ohio to Florida take?

Two days on most lanes. Central Florida and South Florida can run into a third day if the receiver appointment slips, so tell us your must-deliver date.

Do you run reefers south?

Yes. Produce, dairy, and other temperature-controlled freight into Florida and Georgia is a regular booking for us.

Can you handle a drop trailer program?

For steady weekly volume, yes. Ask and we will put together a plan with the carriers who run the lane.
