Ohio to New York

Ohio to New York and Long Island freight.

This is the lane we run more than any other. Since 2024 our team has booked more than 700 truckloads from Ohio into New York, most of them to Long Island receivers in Medford, Hicksville, and Farmingdale, plus the boroughs and upstate. We know which carriers will cross the bridges, which receivers run late, and what the rate has to be to get a truck there on the day you need it.

700+

Loads on the lane

More than 700 Ohio to New York truckloads since 2024, from Clyde, Marion, Columbus, and the Cleveland area. Enough volume that carriers call us looking for the next one.

LI

Long Island is not a problem

Plenty of carriers refuse Long Island. Ours do not. We book drivers who already know the Long Island Expressway, the bridge tolls, and the receiver docks in Suffolk and Nassau.

1 DAY

Transit you can plan around

Roughly 500 to 600 miles. Pick up in Ohio today, deliver in New York tomorrow morning with a single driver.

What shippers on this lane get

A carrier who has been to that dock before.

Appliance, consumer goods, and industrial freight all move on this lane for us. Most receivers want an appointment, some want a liftgate, and a few in the city want a smaller truck. We sort that out before the truck is booked, not at the dock.

Rates on this lane move with the season and with what is coming back out of the Northeast. Because we also book freight out of New Jersey and Pennsylvania, we can often pair the return and keep your rate steady.

Typical freight on this lane

  • Major appliances and consumer durables
  • Palletized consumer goods to Long Island distribution centers
  • Industrial parts and packaging to the five boroughs
  • Retail replenishment to upstate New York
  • Dry van, 53 foot, live or drop as the receiver requires

Questions shippers ask

Straight answers

What does Ohio to New York cost?

It depends on the day, the receiver, and fuel. Send the pickup and delivery ZIPs and we will give you a real number from recent loads, usually within the hour.

Can you deliver to Long Island with an appointment?

Yes. Most of our Long Island loads deliver by appointment. Tell us the window and we will book a carrier who can hold it.

Do you handle New York City deliveries?

Yes, including the boroughs. If the receiver needs a straight truck or a liftgate, say so up front and we will quote the right equipment.

How fast can you cover a load?

Same-day coverage is common on this lane because we have carriers who run it weekly. Next-day pickup is normal; same-day is often possible before noon.
