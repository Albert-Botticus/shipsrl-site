What we need for an LTL quote

Get the details right and the quote holds.

LTL pricing turns on freight class, which comes from density, so weight and dimensions matter more than anything else. Carriers reweigh and reclass freight, and the difference lands on your bill. We would rather ask twice than let that happen.

Residential, liftgate, limited access, inside delivery, and appointment deliveries are all common and all billable. Tell us up front and the quote includes them. Our LTL quote page mirrors the carrier rating screen so nothing gets missed.

Send us

  • Pickup and delivery ZIP codes
  • Pallet count, weight per pallet, and dimensions
  • Freight class or NMFC if you know it
  • Accessorials: liftgate, residential, limited access, appointment
  • Hazmat, stackable, declared value if it applies

Questions shippers ask

Straight answers

Where do you ship LTL from?

Anywhere, but most of our LTL moves in and out of Columbus, Westerville, Groveport, Obetz, and the central Ohio industrial parks.

How is LTL different from a truckload?

LTL means your pallets share a trailer with other shippers' freight and move through carrier terminals. It costs less for small shipments and takes a day or two longer than a dedicated truck.

Can you ship a single pallet?

Yes. One pallet to a residence with a liftgate is a normal LTL shipment for us.

When should I use a full truck instead?

Roughly eight to ten pallets or 10,000 pounds is the crossover point. Above that, a dedicated truck is often cheaper and always faster. We will tell you which one makes sense.
