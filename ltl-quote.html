2 What is shipping

One line per type of handling unit. Weight is the total for that line with the pallet included. Dimensions are optional, but with them we can quote the right class and the rate holds up at delivery.

Total weight: 0 lb
Not sure of your freight class? That is common, and it is fine. Here is how we handle it.

Leave the class on "Not sure" and give us accurate weight and dimensions. Freight class is set by the National Motor Freight Classification (NMFC), and for most goods it comes from density: pounds per cubic foot. As you type, each item shows its estimated density class, and we confirm it before quoting.

LTL carriers can reweigh and re-measure freight after pickup. If their numbers put it in a different class, they bill the corrected class on the final invoice. Accurate dimensions and weight now are the best protection against a surprise later.

Cubic feet = length x width x height in inches, divided by 1,728. Density = total weight divided by cubic feet. The density falls into one of these bands on the NMFC density scale (in effect since July 2025):

Lower density means a higher class and a higher rate, so loose, light or oversized freight costs more per pound than dense freight. Some commodities carry a fixed NMFC class regardless of density because of handling, stowability or liability, so the estimate is a starting point, not a guarantee. If you know your NMFC item number, enter it under the item and we will use it.
