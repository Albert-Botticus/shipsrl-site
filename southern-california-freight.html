California regional

Southern California and the Inland Empire, same day.

Ohio is home, but California is where a large share of our freight actually moves. Since 2024 our team has booked more than 1,200 truckloads inside the state, most of them out of Perris in the Inland Empire and Stockton in the Central Valley. Short runs to Compton and the Los Angeles basin, longer ones up to Hayward, San Jose, and Union City, and steady volume into Tracy, Manteca, Fresno, and West Sacramento. We know the warehouses, the traffic, and which carriers will actually make a 7 AM appointment in Compton.

Why a Midwest agency runs California well

Because we have done it every week for two years.

Most of our California freight is appliances and consumer durables moving from manufacturer distribution centers to retail and last-mile warehouses. That means appointment deliveries, drop trailers at some sites, and receivers who do not wait around. We built a carrier base in the Inland Empire and the Central Valley that handles exactly that.

Because Armstrong Transport Group also books freight out of Southern California every day, our carriers are rarely short a load. That is how we keep a regional rate steady when the port and the import season push the spot market around.

Questions shippers ask

Straight answers

Do you have trucks in the Inland Empire?

Yes. Perris, Riverside, Ontario, and Fontana are where most of our California loads start, and we have carriers based there.

Can you pick up today in Southern California?

Usually, if we hear from you in the morning Pacific time. Regional runs inside the basin are the easiest loads we cover.
