What we look for

Our requirements are the normal ones.

  • Active operating authority and a USDOT number in good standing.
  • Insurance at the standard minimums: $1,000,000 auto liability and $100,000 cargo, with Armstrong Transport Group as certificate holder.
  • Carrier vetting through Armstrong's compliance platform. Setup takes a few minutes and you only do it once.
  • Tracking on the load, either through the app or check calls, so the shipper gets updates without chasing anyone.
  • No double brokering. The carrier we book is the carrier that shows up.
